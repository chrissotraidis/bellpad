# Bellpad app-icon provenance

The opaque 1024×1024 `AppIcon.png` was generated for Bellpad on 2026-08-04
with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool, then resized locally from the
generated 1254×1254 output. No retail game image, extracted asset, official
logo, character, typography, or third-party icon was supplied as an input.

The final prompt preserved an original brass handbell, teal woven handle,
folk-art texture, warm palette, and abstract ring lines while requiring a
full-bleed square with no baked rounded corners, white margins, text, logos,
characters, animals, leaves, houses, trees, furniture, bags, stars, controller
symbols, or recognizable copyrighted game items.

The asset catalog's universal 1024×1024 source is compiled by Apple's `actool`
into the required iPhone and iPad renditions. The macOS build derives its
standard iconset sizes from this same clean source.
